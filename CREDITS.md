[PokéAPI sprite archive](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/tree/master/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-v/black-white/animated)

- Astronomy and zone handling use [Astral](https://github.com/sffjunkie/astral), [tzlocal](https://github.com/regebro/tzlocal) and the [IANA Time Zone Database](https://www.iana.org/time-zones) distributed by Python's `tzdata` package.

The artwork build uses [Pillow](https://python-pillow.org/) and [NumPy](https://numpy.org/). These dependencies are installed from their own packages under their respective licenses.
